Proposed sequence. Every required gate needs measured evidence before promotion. All 23 gate declarations are currently not-run.

  1. 1. Foundation proof

    internal · 4 direct gates · starting proof

    Prove storage, authority, containment and the public build.

    Required evidence
    • File/component origin and rights inventory, distribution mode, notices and data/service terms.
    • Clean public-only build with pinned inputs, effective supply-chain policy and real import boundaries.
    • Desktop and native-mobile key/storage/Artifact encryption, wrong-key denial, transaction and restore proof.
    • Durable command/provenance acceptance, worker filesystem/network denial and current-authority enforcement.

  2. 2. Local alpha

    prerelease · 5 direct gates · after Foundation proof

    One complete accountless workflow on web and Electron.

    Required evidence
    • Accountless General intake, qualified capture, human Finding and cited Report end to end.
    • Portable archive round trip, interrupted migration and crash recovery without lost acknowledged records.
    • Shared budget exhaustion, cancellation, stale attempt denial and disconnected control behavior.
    • The same exposed supported feature slice on web and Electron, truthful unavailable paths.
    • Staffed synthetic-tested private intake, supported versions and sanitized disclosure procedure.

  3. 3. Public beta

    prerelease · 4 direct gates · after Local alpha

    All template scenarios, focused mobile and truthful catalog coverage.

    Required evidence
    • All seven template scenarios and shared acceptance requirements executed against real candidate clients/hosts.
    • Focused iOS/Android paths, accessibility, offline conflict and context-preserving handoff on qualified builds.
    • A truthful implemented/qualified/blocked state and responsible owner for every catalog target.
    • Provider/Runner credential boundaries and active-scope, scope-change and excluded-redirect denial.

  4. 4. Limited managed preview

    prerelease · 1 direct gates · after Public beta

    A separate managed track. Its readiness does not block Local GA.

    Required evidence
    • Access, encryption, restore/deletion, declared U.S. locations, support and cost ceilings before real managed Cases.

  5. 5. Local GA

    ga · 6 direct gates · after Public beta

    The complete approved local scope, including all 33 qualified adapters.

    Required evidence
    • All 33 target adapters independently admitted on declared execution paths with current rights/access evidence.
    • All approved interchange formats, historical provenance and explicit unsupported/unavailable cases.
    • Approved platform matrix, benchmark budgets and accessible complete local feature scope.
    • Exact signed artifacts, seven-day gates, tampered-update rejection, migration recovery and staged rollout proof.
    • Named staffed owners, supported versions, notices, tested upgrades and historical archive readability.
    • A different human approves the exact artifact evidence; no failed/not-run required gate is waived.

  6. 6. Paid managed GA

    ga · 4 direct gates · after Local GA + Limited managed preview

    Separate approval after shared scope and managed operational proof.

    Required evidence
    • Private builds consume immutable reviewed public packages; public builds require no private source/secrets.
    • Entitlements, metering/spend caps, collaboration authority/conflicts and managed deletion/recovery.
    • Measured performance and 30-day reliability, restore/deletion exercises, 24/7 pager plus backup, support, terms and region disclosure.
    • A different human approves the exact artifact evidence; no failed/not-run required gate is waived.
Six policy choices await user review. Local GA and Paid managed GA have separate approvals. No dates, release qualification, license changes, or production actions are implied.
